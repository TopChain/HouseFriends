HouseFriends turns real completed home-service experiences into useful local trust.

SHARE WHAT REALLY HAPPENED
Share the Service Friend, work completed, actual reported cost, month/year, five simple ratings, and a helpful comment.

SEARCH WITHOUT EXPOSING A HOME
Discover experiences around a safe public anchor such as a school, fire station, police station, hospital/urgent care, or place of worship. A public marker is never presented as a private service address.

UNDERSTAND WHAT YOU SEE
HouseFriends clearly labels community-shared experiences, provider self-listed profiles, claimed status, and any future Sponsored placement. Payment never changes ratings, trust badges, or review visibility.

BUILD TWO KINDS OF REPUTATION
Service Friends earn service-quality ratings. Helpful recommenders build separate Trust relationships. When a recommendation leads to another completed service, the existing Helpful relationship upgrades to Verified—it never counts twice.

PRIVACY-FIRST PUBLIC IDENTITY
Every person uses a chosen alias plus a random HF-ID. Google or Apple sign-in names and email addresses are not displayed as public identity.

HouseFriends is a community trust network, not a guarantee of provider suitability, licensing, insurance, pricing, or results. Check regulated-service requirements with the appropriate local authority.
