HouseFriends Terms and Community Rules

Completed experiences only

Share a service experience only after real work occurred. Use the actual reported cost and month/year. Do not post a private address, exact service date, confidential document, or unrelated personal information.

Independent labels and reputation

Community-shared, provider self-listed, Sponsored, claimed, verified, and unknown states are distinct. Payment never changes ratings, trust badges, referral counts, or review visibility.

Fair participation

Do not fabricate jobs, manipulate ratings, farm badges, harass others, or expose personal data. A provider may respond or report but cannot edit another person’s genuine experience. Rewards may recognize a verified referral contribution but may never require positive sentiment, a rating, or review removal.

Regulated work

Users must check licensing, insurance, permits, and suitability with the appropriate local authority. HouseFriends does not claim verification unless expressly labeled.

Moderation

HouseFriends may filter, limit, remove, or preserve content and accounts for privacy, safety, integrity, and legal compliance. Actions are audited and affected users may appeal.

Final legal terms

Before public launch, the operator will add governing law, dispute terms, warranties, liability, payment terms for any activated promotion, age requirements, and jurisdiction-specific notices.
