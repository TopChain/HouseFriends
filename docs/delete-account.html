Delete a HouseFriends account

Do not post account details or identity documents in a public GitHub issue.

Delete in the app

  1. Open HouseFriends and sign in.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Under Account controls, choose Delete account.
  4. Confirm permanent deletion.

This authenticated path removes the account and associated data that HouseFriends is not legally required to retain. Ratings and referral aggregates are recalculated. Genuine experiences authored by other people about a provider entity are not the deleted account’s data and may remain.

If you cannot access the app

A private operator support channel will be added here before public launch so a verified account owner can submit a deletion request from the web. The public release must not begin until that channel and the final operator identity are published.
