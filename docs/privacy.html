HouseFriends Privacy Policy

Release-candidate policy · August 2026

HouseFriends is designed to share the service experience—never the private home.

Public identity

Every person chooses an alias and receives a random HF-ID. HouseFriends does not construct or display a human legal name from Google or Apple sign-in. A company or voluntary trade name may be public as an entity name.

Location

An exact home address or source GPS point is used only ephemerally, when needed, to suggest an approved safe public anchor. It is not persisted or published. Public content uses approved anchors or a coarse locality fallback.

Collected content

Published experience data may include service month/year, actual reported cost and currency, five ratings, comment, safe anchor, category, provider, and public alias/HF-ID. Account identifiers, email, sessions, reports, blocks, appeals, claim evidence, and unapproved business cards are private.

Media

Version 1.0 production does not enable user photo or video uploads. Photo sharing will remain disabled until private storage, server-side processing, moderation, and updated privacy disclosures are deployed.

Your controls

Users can report content, block accounts, correct profile data, appeal moderation, sign out, and initiate deletion inside the app. Deletion removes account-associated data as required and recalculates reputation; genuine experiences about a provider entity may remain with the author.

Service providers and retention

Contracted infrastructure may process data for authentication, database, mapping, moderation, notification, analytics, security, and support. Retention follows operational purpose, legal requirements, fraud prevention, disputes, and user rights.

Launch notice

Before processing real user data, the operator will add the legal entity, mailing address, private contact channel, regional rights, subprocessors, retention schedule, minimum age, and effective date. This release-candidate page is not authorization to launch with real users.
